## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-assurance, clinically rigorous environment crafted specifically for intensive online health consultations, patient longitudinal monitoring, and provider workflow management. The brand identity balances precision medical rigor with empathetic, calming reassurance—counteracting medical anxiety through visual clarity, disciplined hierarchy, and sterile digital elegance.

The target audience comprises two distinct user archetypes:
1. **Clinical Providers & Care Teams**: Requiring information-dense, scannable data layouts, immediate alert triage, immutable SOAP documentation trails, and total certainty regarding data privacy states (internal notes vs. patient-facing summaries).
2. **Patients & Program Participants**: Demanding friction-free mobile check-ins (completed in under two minutes), crystal-clear timeline milestones, legible treatment plans, and unambiguous escalation pathways for urgent symptoms.

Drawing from **Modern Corporate / Clinical Precision**, the aesthetic eschews whimsical decorations in favor of deliberate structure: crisp slate borders, soft sterile surface tiers, eucalyptus and deep teal accents evoking restorative health science, and high-visibility semantic status badges. Medical data surfaces prioritize zero-ambiguity layouts where critical health signals, triage priorities, and confidentiality demarcations are instantly discernible.

## Colors

The palette employs therapeutic cool spectrums paired with high-contrast functional neutrals to create an environment of pristine clarity and medical composure.

### Color Roles & Guidelines
- **Primary (`#0E7490` - Deep Teal)**: Anchor for primary actions, navigation wayfinding, active clinical states, and prominent headers. It conveys clinical depth, authority, and safety.
- **Secondary (`#0D9488` - Eucalyptus)**: Utilized for supportive actions, verified status indicators, milestone progress tracks, and positive recovery markers.
- **Tertiary (`#0369A1` - Cerulean Slate)**: Employed for technical clinical linkages, multi-tenant accents, and secondary analytical charts.
- **Neutrals (`#F8FAFC` to `#0F172A`)**: The background operates on a layered slate paradigm (`#F8FAFC` canvas under `#FFFFFF` clinical cards), preventing eye fatigue during lengthy chart reviews while retaining sterile contrast. Borders rely on `#E2E8F0` for structure without ocular noise.

### Clinical Semantics & Confidentiality Partitioning
- **Emergency / Urgent (`#BE123C` on `#FFE4E6`)**: Reserved strictly for life-safety flags, abnormal lab alerts, and emergency triage bypass prompts (`FR-INT-04`). Never used for aesthetic badges.
- **Needs Review / Warning (`#B45309` on `#FEF3C7`)**: Applied to pending provider reviews, expiring scripts, and borderline vitals.
- **Stable / Verified (`#047857` on `#D1FAE5`)**: Indicates normal clinical parameters, approved check-ins, and locked SOAP records.
- **Dual-Mode Confidentiality Guard**: A dedicated chromatic separation protects against data leakage. Clinician-only **Internal Notes** utilize an Indigo boundary (`#EEF2FF` fill with `#4338CA` badges and `#C7D2FE` borders). In contrast, **Patient-Visible Summaries** use pure white backgrounds with teal verification badges, ensuring staff instantly recognize publication scope before saving.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is built with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for display headers and **Inter** for all clinical body copy, data grids, SOAP notes, and operational controls.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** introduces sculpted, humane warmth in workspace sectioning and milestone banners, ensuring the digital clinic feels approachable rather than intimidating.
- **Inter** provides objective, tall-x-height clarity across patient records, medication dosages, and check-in logs. Its neutral tabular metrics guarantee that vitals (`120/80 mmHg`, `98.6 °F`) remain visually aligned without jitter across streaming telemetry.

### Typographic Principles
1. **SOAP Template Structuring**: Section headings (`Subjective`, `Objective`, `Assessment`, `Plan`) strictly require `title-md` in uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.05em`) paired with `body-md` content for rapid vertical scanning.
2. **Clinical Numbers & Timestamps**: Must always render using tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to align historical check-in dates and metric deltas.
3. **Mobile Readability (360px minimum)**: Headings clamp fluidly into their `-mobile` counterparts on screens `<640px` to protect vertical viewport economy during rapid patient intakes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adapts between two operational paradigms: **Clinical Density** for desktop staff workstations and **Focused Linear Flow** for patient mobile devices.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop Workspaces (Patient 360, Work Queue)**: 12-column fluid responsive grid, maximum container width `1440px`, with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) margins. The layout supports asymmetric split-screen views (e.g., 4-column patient timeline & history alongside 8-column SOAP drafting / consultation video).
- **Tablet Workspaces (`768px` - `1024px`)**: 8-column grid with `1rem` gutters. Collapsible sidebars collapse to icon-only navigation rails.
- **Mobile Patient Flow (`360px` - `640px`)**: Single-column vertical stack with `1rem` (`16px`) page margins and `0.75rem` (`12px`) element gaps. Cards consume 100% of viewport width inside margins to maximize touch targets for intake questions.

### Spacing Rhythm
- Standard spacing follows an 8pt base grid (`space-xs`: 4px, `space-sm`: 8px, `space-md`: 16px, `space-lg`: 24px, `space-xl`: 32px).
- Intra-card component padding defaults to `space-md` for patient check-ins and `space-sm` for high-density clinician table cells.
- Form control gaps strictly adhere to `space-md` (`16px`) to eliminate accidental miss-taps during critical symptom intake.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows and ornamental skeuomorphism, employing **Tonal Layering** and **Low-Contrast Structural Outlines** to preserve a sterile, high-precision clinical presentation.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Surface)**: `#F8FAFC`. The foundational canvas underlay representing ambient clinical space.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation)**: `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a crisp 1px `#E2E8F0` border. Zero shadow under standard resting conditions. Visual elevation is generated solely through crisp perimeter contrast against the slate canvas.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Active State)**: Applied to active patient cards, popovers, and contextual action menus. Uses a subtle clinical shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)` accompanied by a border shift to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Emergency Overlays & Modal Drawers)**: Applied to emergency instruction dialogs (`FR-INT-04`), multi-tenant workspace switchers, and full SOAP signing modals. Elevated using `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` over an intentional `#0F172A` backdrop overlay with 40% opacity.

### Security Watermark & Audit Boundaries
Exported medical records, intake PDFs, and audit trail sidebars incorporate an etched, low-opacity repeat background watermark pattern paired with a reinforced `#CBD5E1` dashed border to visibly signal verified, tamper-evident documents.

## Shapes

The design system implements a controlled `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem / 8px baseline radius), establishing a balanced visual geometry that feels modern and approachable without compromising medical formality.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Input Fields, Buttons, Alert Chips**: `0.5rem` (`8px`) for clean, cohesive component alignment.
- **Clinical Cards, Data Modules, SOAP Blocks**: `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) to group complex clinical observations into neat visual compartments.
- **Emergency Priority Dialogs & Sticky Alerts**: `0.75rem` (`12px`) outer radius with rigid internal structural components to communicate urgency.
- **Triage Badges & Status Pills**: Pill-shaped (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish contextual metadata and severity tags from actionable rectangular buttons.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#0E7490` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius, `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)` shadow. On hover: `#0C4A6E`. Height is `44px` on mobile for strict touch-target accessibility; `38px` in desktop clinical workspaces.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: `#FFFFFF` background with 1px `#E2E8F0` border and `#0F172A` text. On hover: `#F1F5F9`.
- **Emergency Escalation Action**: Solid `#BE123C` background with white text; activates immediate crisis dispatch or direct phone link.

### 2. Clinical Alert Badges & Chips
- Compact, pill-shaped tags (`font-size: 11px`, `font-weight: 600`, padding `2px 8px`).
- **Urgent / High Priority**: `#BE123C` text, `#FFE4E6` background, 1px `#FECDD3` border.
- **Needs Review**: `#B45309` text, `#FEF3C7` background, 1px `#FDE68A` border.
- **Stable / Normal**: `#047857` text, `#D1FAE5` background, 1px `#A7F3D0` border.
- **Internal Only**: `#4338CA` text, `#EEF2FF` background, 1px `#C7D2FE` border.

### 3. Dual-Mode Confidentiality Tabs (Internal Notes vs. Patient Summary)
- Segmented control mounted directly atop the clinical editor.
- **Internal Note Active**: Container background switches to an explicit `#EEF2FF` wash with a 1px `#C7D2FE` boundary and a persistent lock icon (`Private: Clinician Eyes Only`).
- **Patient Summary Active**: Container switches to crisp white `#FFFFFF` with a `#0E7490` boundary and an open eye icon (`Visible: Synced to Patient Portal`).
- The transition between modes is intentionally pronounced to prevent accidental disclosure of private diagnostic working hypotheses.

### 4. Patient Check-In & Symptom Inputs
- **Text Inputs & Selects**: Height `44px`, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, `#FFFFFF` background. Focus state introduces a 2px `#0E7490` ring with a `2px` offset.
- **Rating Scales / Milestone Toggles**: Segmented horizontal card selectors with minimum tap target of `48px × 48px`, providing immediate tactile green (`#0D9488`) selection fill when patients report pain scales (1-10) or adherence status.

### 5. Patient 360 Timeline Milestone Indicator
- A vertical or horizontal track connecting chronological care events.
- Milestone nodes feature an outer circle (`24px`) with a status-driven center dot (`8px`):
  - Completed milestones: `#0D9488` fill with white checkmark.
  - Upcoming check-in: `#FFFFFF` fill with `#0E7490` active pulse ring.
  - Missed check-in: `#B45309` amber warning border.

### 6. Emergency Instruction Banner (`FR-INT-04`)
- Prominently anchored top container utilizing `#FFE4E6` background, 1.5px solid `#BE123C` border, and high-visibility crisis routing copy.
- Persists above all intake queues; cannot be dismissed without explicit confirmation that the patient is not currently experiencing life-threatening symptoms.
